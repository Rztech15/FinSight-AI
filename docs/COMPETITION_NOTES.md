# Competition notes (FinTechathon 2026 – International Track, Topic E: SME Finance Copilot)

Sources: the competition's public pages as read on 2026-10-16/17, mostly third-party mirrors (its.ac.id, ieee-cis-singapore.org). The official site (fintechathon.g-ican.com) could not be fetched directly — **re-check everything there before submitting.**

- **Deadline:** Oct 20, 2026 23:59 Beijing time (UTC+8); submissions can be updated until then.
- **Topic E:** financing-option matching, cash-flow management with alerts, business financial analysis.
- **Judging:** task completion 40%, security & compliance 30%, innovation & interaction 30%.
- **Deliverables:** technical documentation (architecture, core algorithm, security design); deck (≤10 min); optional demo video (≤5 min); source code with README, repo link, deployment instructions, test cases; security self-assessment (permission tiers + known risks); execution evidence (sandbox logs + demo video).
- **Dates (sources disagree):** online review/finalists differ between pages; finals Nov 21–23, Shenzhen.

## ⚠ Eligibility — check before investing more time
Per the pages read: participants must be **full-time students at overseas universities** (not Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan); teams of **2–5**; at least **60% non-Chinese nationals**. Registration is by emailing a form to fintechathon@ican-x.com. Confirm the team meets these rules on the official site.
